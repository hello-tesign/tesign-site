WHY IT MATTERS

The author spells out the reasoning in the README: existing checkers were expensive and overbearing, and everything you wrote was sent to their servers. The main alternative needed gigabytes of RAM. Harper runs locally, so there is no network round-trip while you revise.

BUILD FROM THIS

  • Written in Rust under the Apache-2.0 licence. Because the checking happens locally, it works without a network connection.

WHO IT'S FOR

Anyone writing English email, proposals or documentation, especially where drafts must not be sent to an outside server.

START IN 5 MINUTES

# Paste a sentence at writewithharper.com to see the checks first, then follow the site's guide to connect it to your editor.

CAVEATS

  • English only — it does not check Korean spelling or grammar.
