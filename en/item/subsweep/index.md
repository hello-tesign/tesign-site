## TESIGN TAKE

By aiming narrowly at Australian bank CSVs it becomes something you can use without reading a manual.

## WHY IT MATTERS

Nobody scrolls through twelve months of transactions to pick out the recurring ones. SubSweep takes the CSV you export from your bank's website, separates weekly, monthly, quarterly and yearly charges from everyday spending, totals them per year, and flags services that raised their price since you signed up and charges from the last 14 days that may still be refundable. It says statements are analysed in memory and never stored, and it supports CSV exports from CBA, Westpac, NAB, ANZ, ING, Up and other major Australian banks.

## BUILD FROM THIS

- A once-a-year clean-out of recurring charges before a move or the new year
- A to-do list that leads straight to cancel links for 30+ services and a pre-written refund email
- With Pro, monthly email monitoring of new charges and price changes

## WHO IT'S FOR

- Australian resident — wants the real yearly cost of streaming, gym and software subscriptions
- Student or working-holiday visitor in Australia — clears lingering charges before leaving
- Household budgeter — catches price rises and refundable charges before they slip past
