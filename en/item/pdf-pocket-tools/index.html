WHY IT MATTERS

Merging a few PDFs or reordering pages usually means uploading the document to an online service. PDF Pocket Tools is a single HTML file of about 200KB you open in the browser to merge, split, rotate, reorder and delete pages, and the files never leave your computer. No install, no account, no internet. It sells for one dollar on itch.io, with a free live demo in the browser that does everything up to the point of saving.

BUILD FROM THIS

  • A no-install PDF fixer kept on a shared office PC
  • A habit of merging contracts and receipts without uploading them

WHO IT'S FOR

Office worker
reluctant to upload contracts to a website
Traveller
needs to sort PDFs with no internet

CAVEATS

  • No GitHub repository; no README to verify (itch.io text only)
  • Paid, one dollar; the free demo stops before saving
