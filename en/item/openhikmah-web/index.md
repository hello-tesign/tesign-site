## TESIGN TAKE

Every edge links to its explanation.

## WHY IT MATTERS

Reading linearly makes it hard to see how verses relate. Open Hikmah lets you place a verse on a canvas and expand related verses by root word, theme or contrast.

## BUILD FROM THIS

- Candidates come from shared Arabic roots and embedding similarity; the AI only explains why a link holds and never invents a reference. Search by reference (2:255), keyword or meaning, share a canvas as one URL, and play audio.

## WHO IT'S FOR

- People who study the Qur'an by theme or root.

## START IN 5 MINUTES

```
# Open openhikmah.com, search a verse and place it on the canvas.
```
