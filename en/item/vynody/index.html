WHY IT MATTERS

Players for downloaded music look and behave differently on every device. Vynody aims to give Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS and Android the same experience.

BUILD FROM THIS

  • Auto-filled tags and artwork (AcoustID, MusicBrainz), LRCLIB lyric search, AI-generated word-by-word lyrics, LAN sync of tracks and playlists, connections to Navidrome, Jellyfin, WebDAV and SMB, and a live spectrum display.

WHO IT'S FOR

People who listen to their own music files instead of streaming.

START IN 5 MINUTES

# Get the build for your device from the repository's Releases; Microsoft Store and App Store (macOS, iOS) versions also exist.

CAVEATS

  • Licensed GPL-3.0. The author says it was built with AI-assisted 'vibe coding', and it is a young project with about 130 stars. Which service powers the AI lyric generation is not stated in the README.
