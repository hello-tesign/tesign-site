WHY IT MATTERS

Plenty of people pay for an expensive platform when all they need is prices and alerts. OpenStock isolates that part as open source: a price table, a sector heatmap and a watchlist on one screen.

BUILD FROM THIS

  • TypeScript under AGPL-3.0, offering real-time price tracking, personalised alerts and detailed company insights.

WHO IT'S FOR

Individual investors watching a handful of tickers who mainly want an alert at a certain price.

START IN 5 MINUTES

# Try the public demo at openstock-ods.vercel.app first, then follow the repository guide to self-host if you want to keep it.

CAVEATS

  • Not a brokerage and not financial advice; market data may be delayed depending on the provider's rules and your configuration. Under AGPL-3.0, deploying a modified version as a web service means publishing your source.
