WHY IT MATTERS

A trip to shoot the sky is wasted if clouds roll in. Nightwatch works out the clear window, the Moon and darkness, alerts you an hour before sunset, and lists targets that are up in that window.

BUILD FROM THIS

  • Tonight's plan with best times and an altitude chart, a week-ahead view of up to ten nights, favourites, and catalogues of nebulae, galaxies and clusters. Astronomy is computed locally and only forecast and sky-survey requests leave your Mac.

WHO IT'S FOR

Amateur astronomers who image the sky with a small telescope.

START IN 5 MINUTES

# Download Nightwatch.dmg from the README and drag it to Applications.

CAVEATS

  • macOS 14 or later only. A brand-new project with 2 stars that depends on external forecast services.
