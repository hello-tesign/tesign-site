WHY IT MATTERS

A single short video involves character design, shot breakdown, asset generation and editing. ArcReel joins those into one pipeline you can pause and resume. Reference images are reused across shots so faces stay consistent, and any single asset can be regenerated or rolled back.

BUILD FROM THIS

  • Python, AGPL-3.0. Content analysis, asset management, storyboarding, media generation, cost tracking and export are organised as one auditable pipeline, with the models configured by you and spend tracked per item.

WHO IT'S FOR

People regularly producing story adaptations, narration videos or product clips, and teams that want a draft in hand before commissioning one.

START IN 5 MINUTES

# Read the quick-start section in the docs at arc-reel.com, confirm it is a self-hosted setup, then prepare a server.

CAVEATS

  • Self-hosted, and the AI models it calls are billed separately. Docs and community are mostly in Chinese. Under AGPL-3.0, offering a modified version as a service means publishing your source too.
