## TESIGN TAKE

Agents can draw with it too, by stating relationships instead of computing coordinates.

## WHY IT MATTERS

Mermaid and friends choose the layout for you, and draw.io means dragging every box by hand. reladraw sits between: the convenience of text with the freedom to place things.

## BUILD FROM THIS

- You write boxes and connections as text and place them relative to each other. It runs in the browser, and the repository includes examples such as a server architecture diagram.

## WHO IT'S FOR

- Developers and planners who draw architecture diagrams often.
