WHY IT MATTERS

Weather sites tell you what Lisbon is like in June, not where it is good this month. SeasonMap marks what is in season and out of season on the map for the month you pick, and says why.

BUILD FROM THIS

  • Pick a month and an activity such as city walking, and places on the world map change colour for in-season or out-of-season; the site lists 293 well-known places in season for September city walking.

WHO IT'S FOR

Travellers choosing where and when to go.

START IN 5 MINUTES

# Open seasonmap.app, pick a month and an activity, and press 'Open the map'.

CAVEATS

  • Per the site, everything is open for the first five minutes with no account; there is a separate pricing page. Source availability and licence could not be confirmed.
