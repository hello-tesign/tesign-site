WHY IT MATTERS

A draft written by a model reads smoothly but keeps its own verbal habits, and readers spot them first. humanizer is an agent skill that finds and removes those traces at the draft stage, attached to Claude Code, Codex or Cursor. It is written in Python under the MIT license and has its own skill distribution page. What counts as an AI tell varies by genre, so read the result yourself.

BUILD FROM THIS

  • An in-house polishing step every draft passes before publishing
  • A post-processing rule that pulls drafts back to the brand voice
