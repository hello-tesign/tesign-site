WHY IT MATTERS

To work a satellite on the radio, or just to see the ISS, you need to know when it passes and where to look. Look4Sat computes passes for more than 9,000 satellites from Celestrak and SatNOGS data, relative to your own position, and the maths runs offline — useful in a field with no signal.

BUILD FROM THIS

  • Kotlin and Jetpack Compose. There is a satellite list, upcoming passes, a polar radar view and a map, searchable by name or NORAD catalogue number. It predicts up to ten days ahead, shows pass progress with transceiver details, and accepts custom TLE files. It is ad-free and says it always will be.

WHO IT'S FOR

Radio amateurs, anyone trying to spot the ISS with the naked eye, and astronomy clubs.

START IN 5 MINUTES

# Install from Google Play, then set your station position in Settings — the predictions depend on it.

CAVEATS

  • Android only. A weekly TLE refresh is recommended for reliable predictions, and results are meaningless until you set your position. GPL-3.0: a modified build must be released under the same terms.
