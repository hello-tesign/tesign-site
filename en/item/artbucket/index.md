## TESIGN TAKE

The web app uses only the public API, so anything it does can be scripted.

## WHY IT MATTERS

Which logo is current, and may this photo run in paid social in Germany? Artbucket keeps assets, rules, rights and releases together and answers 'May I use this?'.

## BUILD FROM THIS

- A web app, MCP server, REST API, CLI and Git share the same data. npx artbucket check reports an expired licence and the replacement asset, and agents connect over MCP with a chosen permission (Suggest, Read or Edit).

## WHO IT'S FOR

- Marketing and design teams who manage brand assets and usage rights.
