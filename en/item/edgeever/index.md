## TESIGN TAKE

It can reportedly run within Cloudflare's free quotas with no server purchase.

## WHY IT MATTERS

Switching note apps is hard when prices rise or exports are a chore. EdgeEver keeps the familiar Evernote-style three-pane layout, lets you own your data, and says it supports AI agents through MCP.

## BUILD FROM THIS

- A three-pane editor with notebooks, tags and search, plus tables, code, formulas, diagrams, backlinks and an outline. Clients for macOS, Windows, Linux, iOS and Android and a web clipper are advertised, and there is a live demo.

## WHO IT'S FOR

- People who want to keep their notes under their own control, or leave Evernote.
