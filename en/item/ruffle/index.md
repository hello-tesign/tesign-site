## TESIGN TAKE

The point is that the original file opens as it is — no conversion, no rebuild. If you still have Flash material sitting in a folder, open one and see.

## WHY IT MATTERS

When Flash ended, twenty years of games, animations and teaching material stopped opening. Ruffle is a reimplementation of the Flash Player in Rust, so the same SWF files run again in a browser — not Adobe’s code with a new wrapper, but a fresh implementation.

## BUILD FROM THIS

- Written in Rust, targeting the desktop and the web through WebAssembly. Pick a file on the web demo and it plays; there are also desktop builds, a browser extension, a version you embed in your own site, and an Android build in the repository. ActionScript 1, 2 and 3 are supported to a large degree.

## WHO IT'S FOR

- Anyone with old Flash teaching material or promos to revive, and people who want to play the games again.
