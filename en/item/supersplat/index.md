## TESIGN TAKE

3D tools usually start with a heavy install; this one starts with a URL, and takes you through to something you can show other people.

## WHY IT MATTERS

A Gaussian splat reconstructed from photographs arrives full of stray points and noise. SuperSplat opens that result, lets you delete what does not belong, line it up, shrink it and publish it — all in the browser, with nothing to download or install.

## BUILD FROM THIS

- Built by PlayCanvas on web technology: a scene list, position/rotation/scale controls, several selection tools and a histogram of the splat data, all in one screen, with export and publish at the end. It is localised into several languages and can also be run locally from source.

## WHO IT'S FOR

- People working with 3D scans, teams showing a space or product in 3D, and anyone teaching the subject.

## START IN 5 MINUTES

```
# Open superspl.at/editor and drag a Gaussian splat (.ply) file in.
```

## CAVEATS

- It is for Gaussian splats, not a general 3D model editor (no obj/fbx workflow), and a large scene asks a lot of your machine’s graphics.
