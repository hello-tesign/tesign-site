## WHY IT MATTERS

Tax-reform debate is usually about averages, not your own home. Tax Map lets you enter a postcode or area, adjust the rate and see the estimated change.

## BUILD FROM THIS

- Shades 296 council areas on a map with yearly or monthly changes, optional Stamp Duty and your own figures. It uses official data from ONS, HM Land Registry and VOA/HMRC and needs no API keys.

## WHO IT'S FOR

- Anyone curious how a property-tax reform could affect a home in England.
