WHY IT MATTERS

A small shop needs filming, casting and editing for even one ad video. Give the agent a product photo and a brief and SuperCMO has it extract product details, pick suitable image and video models, cast AI actors and edit the result.

BUILD FROM THIS

  • A set of skills installed into agents such as Claude, Cursor, Codex and Hermes. The README shows example requests: a get-ready-with-me reel for a T-shirt, a 3D cartoon ad for a tumbler, an ad video for an energy drink. Install with one line: npx github:SupercmoHQ/superCMO-skills --all.

WHO IT'S FOR

Small business owners and marketers who have product photos but no capacity to produce ad videos.

START IN 5 MINUTES

# Install into your agent with npx github:SupercmoHQ/superCMO-skills --all, then describe the ad you want along with a product photo.

CAVEATS

  • The fees for the AI image and video models that produce the output, and the output quality, could not be confirmed here. Recently released, with 62 stars.
