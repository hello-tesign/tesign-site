WHY IT MATTERS

Some drivers have an iPhone but an Android-based car screen with no CarPlay. DiPlay is installed on the car and shows your iPhone's maps, music and messages, and says it needs no jailbreak, dongle, Mac or account.

BUILD FROM THIS

  • Wired USB and wireless CarPlay, BYD dashboard guidance arrows, distance and street names on verified firmware, car hotspot support, and icon, text, resolution and frame-rate settings. The app is available in English, Chinese, Arabic, Russian and Spanish.

WHO IT'S FOR

iPhone users who drive a BYD.

START IN 5 MINUTES

# Install it on the car, not the iPhone, following the Install and connect guide on the website.

CAVEATS

  • Licensed GPL-3.0. Not an Apple-certified product: it bundles an experimental accessory identity recovered from public Carlinkit firmware, and the README says its private key is extractable. Other brands are unsupported, the head unit must allow APK installs, this is a public preview (0.2.8) with occasional audio cutouts, and DRM video such as Apple TV+ is not supported.
