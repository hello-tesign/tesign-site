WHY IT MATTERS

Installing or signing up for a paid design tool just to draw one logo or icon is a lot. Scissor lets you draw vectors and pixels together in a single browser tab, and says nothing you make is uploaded.

BUILD FROM THIS

  • Vector tools with pen, shapes, live effects and full path editing, plus pixel layers painted beside the vector art. Properties, colour and type panels sit on the right, and your work stays on your device. A terms-of-use dialog appears on first open.

WHO IT'S FOR

Small business owners and designers who need a quick logo, icon or image.
