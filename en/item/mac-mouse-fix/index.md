## TESIGN TAKE

There are other mouse utilities, but this one has over ten thousand stars and a level of polish that mirrors Apple's own trackpad gestures closely.

## WHY IT MATTERS

macOS gives trackpads great gestures but leaves mice out. Mac Mouse Fix lets any USB or Bluetooth mouse assign trackpad-level actions — Mission Control, Spaces, Smart Zoom — to its scroll wheel and extra buttons.

## BUILD FROM THIS

- A single preferences app that lives in the menu bar. A per-button mapping screen lets you assign click, scroll and drag actions, with adjustable smooth-scrolling and zoom sensitivity.

## WHO IT'S FOR

- Mac users who want trackpad-level convenience from a cheap mouse, or who want mouse buttons mapped their own way.

## START IN 5 MINUTES

```
# Download the app from macmousefix.com, grant Accessibility permission when prompted, and assign actions in the Buttons tab.
```
