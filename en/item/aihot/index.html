WHY IT MATTERS

Only people inside an industry — law, HR, finance — know which news matters. The author runs the AI hot-topics site aihot.news and open-sourced all of it: the site, back office, selection pipeline, prompts and pass thresholds, so anyone can retarget it to their own field.

BUILD FROM THIS

  • It collects from RSS, web pages, APIs, X and official accounts, keeps one copy of each item, pre-screens with a large language model, then scores twice independently and keeps only what clears the bar. It writes titles and summaries in Chinese, clusters reports of the same event, and publishes a daily digest at 08:00 plus weekly and monthly reports. You change behaviour by editing the prompts in industry/prompts/.

WHO IT'S FOR

People who want to run a news-digest site for their own industry.

START IN 5 MINUTES

# Follow the README's run guide to start the repository, then change the sources and prompts to fit your industry.

CAVEATS

  • Docs and the site are Chinese-first (the README is in Chinese). It relies on a large language model, so you must arrange model access and cost yourself; which model and what cost could not be confirmed here.
