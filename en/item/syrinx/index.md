## TESIGN TAKE

Having the piano under you is what keeps a piece moving when you practise alone, and the five bundled songs mean you can start without hunting for sheet music.

## WHY IT MATTERS

Practising alone, it is hard to tell whether the tempo slipped or the pitch was true. Syrinx follows the score with a cursor while a sampled piano plays the accompaniment, then lets you replay the recording with a note-by-note pitch comparison.

## BUILD FROM THIS

- An Electron and React desktop app. It imports MusicXML, XML and MXL, reads the title, part and time signature, and keeps everything in folders on your own device. A four-beat count-in starts the accompaniment, clicking a bar jumps there, and tempo moves between 0.5x and 1.5x without changing pitch. Five pieces ship with accompaniment and artwork.

## WHO IT'S FOR

- People practising flute on their own, and anyone keeping a piece alive between lessons.
