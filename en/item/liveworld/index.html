WHY IT MATTERS

Live cameras on cities, beaches and harbours are everywhere but scattered. LiveWorld gathers them on a globe so you can spin to a region and open a feed.

BUILD FROM THIS

  • Cameras sit on a satellite-imagery globe as numbered clusters, with search by camera, city or country, a random jump (the dice), saved cameras, submissions and ambient music. Per the site, one person built it and keeps 2,418 cameras online.

WHO IT'S FOR

Anyone wanting a view of another city on a break, or a preview of a place before travelling.

START IN 5 MINUTES

# Open liveworld.info and spin the globe, or press the dice for a random camera.

CAVEATS

  • Per the site's cookie notice, it shows a few ads. The feeds are YouTube live streams, so a camera disappears when its stream stops. Source availability and licence could not be confirmed.
