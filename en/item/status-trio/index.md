## TESIGN TAKE

It does one thing, which makes the decision easy: if your menu bar is crowded the effect is immediate, and if it is not, you do not need this.

## WHY IT MATTERS

The menu bar is always short on space. Taking its cue from the iPhone's combined Wi-Fi, battery and cellular indicator, this puts three signals into one icon — with volume in place of cellular data.

## BUILD FROM THIS

- A native macOS app in Swift under Apache-2.0. Icon size is configurable from 16 to 36 pt (28 pt by default), and you can opt to show the standard Wi-Fi icon for Ethernet.

## WHO IT'S FOR

- Mac users with a crowded menu bar, especially on smaller laptop screens.
