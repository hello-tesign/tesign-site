## TESIGN TAKE

It began for Sri Lankan government schools, but the shape of it travels. The point is giving a public institution a way to run this without handing its records to a foreign commercial service.

## WHY IT MATTERS

Many schools still run on paper and a handful of disconnected tools, which makes information hard to find, update and share safely. OpenSchool brings that work into one system a school can host itself, keeping student and administrative data under local control.

## BUILD FROM THIS

- A Go and Gin backend covering academic years, grades, classes and streams, student, staff and guardian records, daily attendance, curriculum and term marks with promotions, timetable planning and publishing, in-app notifications, reports and dashboards — with separate portals for administrators, teachers, students and parents. No licence fees, no vendor lock-in.

## WHO IT'S FOR

- School administrators, public-sector and education bodies needing their own system, and people building education software.
