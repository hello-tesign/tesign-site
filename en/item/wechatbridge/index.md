## TESIGN TAKE

It builds a path out of WeChat on top of WeChat's own forward menu, which is the practical part.

## WHY IT MATTERS

Since macOS WeChat 4.1.13 you can merge-forward several chats to another app, but only apps with a share extension appear in that list. WeChatBridge fills that gap so one forward lands in Codex, Claude, Obsidian and more.

## BUILD FROM THIS

- A native macOS app that receives WeChat's forwarded bundle (a ZIP of text, images and video) and hands it to the targets you enable — Codex, Claude, Doubao, Qwen and others — with everything processed on your own machine.

## WHO IT'S FOR

- Mac users who work over WeChat and want those conversations summarised by AI or kept in their notes.

## START IN 5 MINUTES

```
# Install the notarized DMG from GitHub Releases, enable the targets in the app, then pick chats in WeChat and merge-forward them.
```

## CAVEATS

- macOS only, and needs macOS WeChat 4.1.13 or later. The app and docs are Chinese-first, though the README has an English version.
