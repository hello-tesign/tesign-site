## TESIGN TAKE

Manufacturing ERPs are usually expensive and closed; this one opens its core under the AGPL so you can install it yourself.

## WHY IT MATTERS

Even small manufacturers end up with quotes, orders, work orders and quality records scattered across spreadsheets and separate tools. Carbon is a manufacturing operating system aimed at complex assembly, contract manufacturing, high volume and configure-to-order work, with its full source open.

## BUILD FROM THIS

- A web app covering sales (customers, RFQs, quotes, orders, invoices) alongside production (MES) and quality (QMS). Use it as a managed cloud service or self-host it.

## WHO IT'S FOR

- Small and mid-sized manufacturers doing make-to-order, assembly or contract work, and people who run their own internal systems.

## START IN 5 MINUTES

```
# Follow the self-hosting guide at carbon.ms/self-hosted, or start on the managed cloud from carbon.ms.
```

## CAVEATS

- The core is AGPL-3.0, but the Enterprise files (packages/ee and any file with .ee. in its name) are under a separate commercial licence, and neither licence covers the Carbon name or logos. The managed cloud is priced per user per month.
