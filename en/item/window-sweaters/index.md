## TESIGN TAKE

It is simply charming, and needs no better reason.

## WHY IT MATTERS

An app for mood rather than utility: it adds a bit of warmth to the windows you stare at all day.

## BUILD FROM THIS

- It draws a knitted pattern around each window, in two styles: patterns in each app's colours (Finder, Claude, Codex, Spotify, Notion, Figma and more) or a zigzag in the app colour with cream.

## WHO IT'S FOR

- Mac users who like to decorate their desktop.
