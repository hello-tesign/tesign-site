## TESIGN TAKE

More important than the 'AI office' label are the byte-preserving save and the command line; together they give coding agents a way to produce real Office files instead of Markdown approximations.

## WHY IT MATTERS

Office alternatives are plenty, but 'open, save, formatting breaks' is the usual failure; GenOffice avoids it by rewriting only the blocks you edited and copying everything else byte-for-byte. An AI panel sits beside every document: edits arrive as tracked changes in Docs and as live formulas (SUMIF, for example) in Sheets, and each AI turn is a snapshot you can roll back. PDF to Word/Excel/PowerPoint conversion and OCR of scans (macOS and Windows) run on-device, and the model is either a Genspark sign-in or your own key for Claude, OpenAI, Gemini, DeepSeek and others, local servers included.

## BUILD FROM THIS

- With the bundled `genoffice` command line and agent skill, Claude Code, Codex, Cursor and others can create and check real .docx/.xlsx/.pptx files without opening a window — monthly reports, quotes, slide drafts. The HTML app proposes a design brief first, then builds a single-file landing page you can export to Word or PDF.

## WHO IT'S FOR

- Individuals and small teams handling .docx/.xlsx/.pptx without Microsoft Office — open, edit and save with the original formatting intact
- People who want AI to do document work but review it — tracked changes, diffs, rollback, answers with cell citations
- Developers automating documents with coding agents — create, convert and check files headless via the `genoffice` CLI and skill
