## TESIGN TAKE

It is non-destructive: it never writes to your original photos or Lightroom catalog.

## WHY IT MATTERS

Working on RAW photos often means a subscription app. RAWmakase opens RAW files from almost every current camera, develops them with Lightroom-style controls and exports JPEG or 16-bit TIFF.

## BUILD FROM THIS

- White balance, exposure and tone, curves, an HSL colour mixer, three-way grading, denoise and sharpening, crop and lens corrections. It imports ratings, flags, keywords and compatible settings from a Lightroom Classic catalog without writing to the original.

## WHO IT'S FOR

- Photographers who shoot RAW and want to edit without a subscription.

## START IN 5 MINUTES

```
# Download the latest version from the repository or rawmakase.com and install it.
```

## CAVEATS

- A personal project in active development that aims for close, not exact, Lightroom parity. Sigma Foveon, Leica Monochrom and already-demosaiced linear DNGs are not supported, and spot removal and masks are experimental.
