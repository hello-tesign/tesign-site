WHY IT MATTERS

The work lives in Jira and Confluence, but AI tools cannot see inside them. This server bridges the two, so a request like "summarise what's left in this sprint" is answered from the real data. It is built and hosted by Atlassian itself.

BUILD FROM THIS

  • Apache-2.0, cloud-hosted by Atlassian, covering Jira, Confluence, Jira Service Management, Bitbucket, Compass, Loom and wider platform data through the Teamwork Graph.

WHO IT'S FOR

Teams on Jira or Confluence who want AI tools in the loop, and managers who have been copying ticket status by hand.

START IN 5 MINUTES

# Open the getting-started guide at atlassian.com/platform/rovo-mcp to connect it to your AI tool, and check first whether your admin has to approve it.

CAVEATS

  • Atlassian Cloud only. Company accounts may need admin approval, and since the connected AI tool can read work data, check the permission scope first.
