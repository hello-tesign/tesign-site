WHY IT MATTERS

Every search leaves a trail that feeds ads and recommendations. SearXNG asks many search services on your behalf, returns only the results, and says users are neither tracked nor profiled.

BUILD FROM THIS

  • The documentation lists up to 261 search engines, optional JavaScript and cookies, use over Tor, 58 translations, and self-hosting on your own server.

WHO IT'S FOR

People who don't want a search history, and admins who want to run their own search service.
