## TESIGN TAKE

Similar services usually add a watermark or lock styles behind a paywall; this one is fully free and MIT-licensed.

## WHY IT MATTERS

Dressing up a product screenshot for social posts or slides by hand is tedious. Shotcandy shows the styled result the moment you paste, and every style preview uses your own image rather than a sample.

## BUILD FROM THIS

- It runs entirely in the browser, so nothing is uploaded. Screen recordings become share-ready videos too. No account, no paywall, no watermark.

## WHO IT'S FOR

- Marketers, designers and solo founders who share product screenshots often.
