About TESIGN Radar

TESIGN Radar collects newly released SaaS, AI apps, developer tools and design experiments from public sources every day, rejects most of them, and publishes only the few worth trying, installing, sharing or building from. Machines filter the noise; a person decides (the first lists of 13–14 September 2026 were AI-chosen and are under review). Run by tesign, a one-person studio. There is no paid placement.

Collect broadly. Reject aggressively. Publish sparingly. Thousands are seen each day; ten are kept.

Apart from the first lists, every published item was seen and kept by a person. AI is used only to thin the candidates; a human decides what is shown.

"First seen" is a real system timestamp. Items reconstructed from historical data are never shown as live discoveries, and there is no paid placement.

The first lists published on 13–14 September 2026 are an exception. The operator delegated that selection to AI and is re-reviewing the items in order, so any of them may change or come down.
