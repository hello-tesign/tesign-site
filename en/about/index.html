How ranks are computed

Rising requires at least 200 observed new stars in the selected window, or at least 100 total stars and an increase of 20% or more. Sort order is the window’s increase. Repositories created within 30 days get NEW. A 24-hour increase of at least 30% with only a GitHub signal gets SPIKE.

All-time rank = stars × survival × cross. Survival is 1.0 for a push within 90 days of the reference time, or 0.8 within 365 days. Cross is 1 + 0.1 × (distinct source count − 1), capped at 1.5. At least 1,000 stars are required; the library category is excluded. Only raw numbers and ranks are displayed.

Both rankings exclude archived repositories, forks, filtered, discarded or merged products, and AI filter verdicts. The reference time is the latest stored GitHub hour. Increases are collected observations, not a guarantee of every star event.
