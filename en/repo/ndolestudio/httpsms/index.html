README EXCERPT

httpSMS httpSMS is a service that lets you use your Android phone as an SMS Gateway to send and receive SMS messages. You make a request to a simple HTTP API and it triggers your Android phone to send an SMS. SMS messages received on your android phone can also be forwarded to your webhook endpoint.
