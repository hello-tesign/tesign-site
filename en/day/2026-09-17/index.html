10 THINGS WORTH SEEING TODAY

Ten today. BG0 removes a background right in the browser with no account, ZapFast rebuilds WhatsApp as a light native client, Wealthfolio keeps net worth on your own device, page-mascot is a cursor-following mascot widget, Podroid boots a real Linux VM on your phone, Capsule packs an app into one file, SparkyFitness is a self-hosted family health tracker, Inkstone is a notebook you deploy on your own Cloudflare account, MartinLoop puts a budget and a verifier around a coding agent, and OpenHunterAI is an alpha security tool for targets you're authorized to test — half of them open right away with nothing to install.

↓
  1. 01BG0Removes a photo's background entirely inside your browser — no upload, no server, no account.
  2. 02ZapFastA from-scratch Rust WhatsApp client with no browser engine — about 150MB idle RAM on Linux, versus 1.13GB for WhatsApp Web.
  3. 03WealthfolioAn open-source portfolio tracker that keeps investments, net worth and spending entirely on your device — no cloud account.
  4. 04page-mascotA tiny React component that puts a character on your page which turns its head to follow the cursor and reacts when clicked.
  5. 05PodroidBoots a real, separately-kernelled Alpine Linux VM on an unrooted Android phone, running Podman, Docker and LXC exactly as they behave on a server.
  6. 06CapsuleA desktop player that packs an HTML app and its data into one SQLite file — no account, no server, just a file to share.
  7. 07SparkyFitnessA self-hosted health tracker for nutrition, exercise, sleep, fasting, mood and body metrics — all on a server you run yourself.
  8. 08InkstoneA self-hosted Markdown notebook you deploy on your own Cloudflare Workers account, with realtime sync, search and backlinks built in.
  9. 09MartinLoopWraps a coding agent with a spend cap and a verifier, so "I'm done" comes with an evidence-backed receipt instead of just a claim.
  10. 10OpenHunterAIAn alpha-stage local workspace that runs attacker-style security assessments — strictly against public targets you own or are authorized to test.
