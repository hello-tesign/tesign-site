10 THINGS WORTH SEEING TODAY

Today's list leans towards tools that keep your material on your own machine: a budgeting app and a grammar checker that run locally, and a scanner that shows what your browser leaks. The other half is groundwork for handing longer work to AI — memory, workspaces, and a bridge to where the work already lives. Licences and limits are stated as they are.

↓
  1. 01Actual BudgetA local-first budgeting app that keeps your finances on your own device, using envelope budgeting.
  2. 02HarperAn English grammar checker that runs on your own machine, so what you write never leaves it.
  3. 03ArcReelA self-hosted workbench that turns a novel or script into characters, scenes, storyboards and finished short videos.
  4. 04OpenStockAn open-source dashboard for tracking stock prices, company details and personalised alerts in one place.
  5. 05Status TrioA native macOS menu bar app that merges Wi-Fi, battery and volume into a single icon.
  6. 06liarjsReads what a browser says about itself, compares it with what the network actually saw, and reports every disagreement.
  7. 07MemOSA memory layer for LLM agents that unifies storing, retrieving and managing long-term memory.
  8. 08CoderA self-hosted platform that moves development environments off laptops onto your own infrastructure, started on demand.
  9. 09MemohAn open-source platform that gives every AI agent its own cloud computer, running even when your laptop is closed.
  10. 10Atlassian MCP ServerAtlassian's official bridge that lets AI tools read and act on Jira, Confluence and the rest of the platform.
